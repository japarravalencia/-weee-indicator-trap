2. Asymmetric Leverage Hierarchy (Sobol Policy-Space Decomposition, N = 1,280)

Why digital transparency alone stalls while circular firm diffusion controls physical recovery.

Lever 1: Anti-Trap Audits (τrep, wgam)

Explains 22.6% of Indicator Trap variance (cutting complacency 80.8%), yet only 2.0% of terminal WEEE variance because linear decommissioning lags (τlin = 2.5 yr) remain intact.

Lever 2: Circular Firm Diffusion (αp, βc)

Dominates 100% of WEEE variance, 95.9% of active park recovery, 96.2% of valorization, and 88.3% of Trap Index variance by structurally extinguishing linear gaming.

Lever 3: Tech Reinvestment (φreinv)

Acts as a targeted economic accelerator (4.6% of active park variance), enabling Run 567 to rebuild installed capacity to 1,038.3 t even under −30% metal price shocks.
